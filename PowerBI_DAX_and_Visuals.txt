Power BI DAX Measures
=====================

Total Sales = SUM(Sales_Data[Sales_INR])

Total Profit = SUM(Sales_Data[Profit_INR])

Total Orders = DISTINCTCOUNT(Sales_Data[Order_ID])

Units Sold = SUM(Sales_Data[Units_Sold])

Profit Margin % = DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total Sales], 0)

Average Order Value = DIVIDE([Total Sales], [Total Orders], 0)

Power BI visuals to create
--------------------------
1. Cards: Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, Profit Margin %.
2. Line chart: Order_Date (Month) on X-axis, Total Sales on Y-axis.
3. Clustered bar chart: Region on Y-axis, Total Sales and Total Profit as values.
4. Donut chart: Category as Legend, Total Sales as Values.
5. Table: Product, Units_Sold, Total Sales, Total Profit.
6. Slicers: Order_Date, Region, Category.
7. Format currency measures as Indian Rupees (₹) and Profit Margin as percentage.

Power BI setup
--------------
Home > Get Data > Text/CSV > select sales_data.csv > Load.
In Model view, ensure Order_Date is Date type and numeric columns are numeric.
Create each measure above using Modeling > New measure.
